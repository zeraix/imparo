# Community Code of Conduct

Imparo welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages,
and hardware. Asking a basic question, reporting a failed experiment, or
disagreeing with a design should be safe here.

## How we work together

- Be respectful and patient. Help newcomers find the information they need.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence, not the person behind them.
- Welcome technical disagreement and explain your reasoning. Leave room to change
  your mind when measurements or new information change the picture.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries. Do not pressure volunteers for
  immediate responses or more work than they can offer.
- Give credit, represent results honestly, and correct mistakes constructively.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private
information without their permission.

## Scope

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project-managed community spaces, as well as when someone officially represents
Imparo. It applies to maintainers and contributors equally. Respectful criticism
of the project or its technical decisions is welcome.

## Reporting and resolution

Report concerns privately to [fergus@zeraix.com](mailto:fergus@zeraix.com), with
links or context you are comfortable sharing. You do not have to confront the
person involved or make the report public.

Maintainers will review the context, limit sharing to people needed to handle the
report, and explain the outcome where appropriate without exposing private
details. Responses should be proportionate: clarification or a warning where
suitable, removal of harmful content, and temporary or permanent participation
restrictions for serious or repeated harm. Retaliation for a good-faith report is
not acceptable.

If you believe a decision missed important context, reply through the same
private channel and request a review. A maintainer directly involved in a dispute
should step aside from deciding its outcome where another maintainer is available.

Thank you for making Imparo a place where people can learn and build together.
